Questions about SI tools and workflow automation.

Does this run on my own equipment?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send or change things without me?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.

Do you only build Custom GPTs?

No. They are only one option. A simple script, automation, or dashboard may fit the job better.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Usually. I can connect files, CRMs, online stores, email, chat tools, and cloud data when access is available.

Can you put unused ChatGPT seats to work?

Yes. Many jobs use the ChatGPT or Claude seat you already pay for around files and tools you already have. A Hermes Desk is one option when an agent on your Mac fits.
