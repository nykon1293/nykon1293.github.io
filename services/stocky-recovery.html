CSVs are a backup. They are not an OS.

Stocky was the place that told the team what to order, what was short, and what the supplier last said. A folder of exports does not do that. I turn the dump into a weekly view you can actually run — using Sheets and the ChatGPT or Claude seat you already pay for. Bring whatever you exported. We separate facts you still have from the operating knowledge that lived only in Stocky.

Usually still usable

What the export tends to keep

  • Product and variant SKUs
  • Vendor names
  • Purchase order history, if you exported it
  • On-hand counts, if you exported them
  • Shopify still has products, orders, and locations
Often missing

What frequently never left Stocky

  • Supplier notes: lead times, “don’t call Friday,” pack rules
  • Par levels / min-max that lived in Stocky fields
  • The weekly cadence Stocky used to enforce
  • Tribal knowledge that never had a column

What a usable weekly view looks like.

One place to run the week. Not a new inventory SaaS. A working sheet plus a short cadence: what is short, what is already inbound, what the supplier said, what to draft this week.

If pars never exported, we reconstruct what we can from history, side sheets, and the people who still remember — then give those numbers a home so they stop living in Slack.
