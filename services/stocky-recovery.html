Usually still usable

What the export tends to keep

  • Product and variant SKUs
  • Vendor names
  • Purchase order history, if you exported it

Often missing

What frequently never left Stocky

  • Supplier notes: lead times, “don’t call Friday,” pack rules
  • Par levels / min-max that lived in Stocky fields
  • The weekly cadence Stocky used to enforce
  • Tribal knowledge that never had a column
