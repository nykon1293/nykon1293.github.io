# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and operations leaders with messy work — scattered spreadsheets, AI stuck in test mode, no trusted view of the business — who might hire Yonatan for consulting or contract work. They arrive comparing operators, not browsing a product. South Florida or remote. Tutoring and project-help visitors exist, but they are not who the first screen is for.

## Product Purpose

This site exists to turn a first visit into a conversation. Success is trust in the first moments, then a clear path to start a project (form, email, or LinkedIn). Named offers should stay concrete — Hermes Agent setup, dashboards, ecommerce ops — not generic “agentic” hype. The work examples and operational numbers have to feel like a working operator, not slideware.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, precise, unhurried. Calm competence with a sharp operator edge. Not hype, not agency-slick. First-person, practical voice: understand the work, then build what people can actually use.

## Anti-references

Generic AI agency sites: purple gradients, glowing orbs, “agentic” hype, and novelty-first copy. Also the default SaaS landing template (pill nav + metric cards + identical icon cards) when it starts to look like costume rather than craft.

## Design Principles

- Show the system, don’t sell the buzzword.
- Calm on the surface, sharp in the details.
- Proof over claims — operational evidence beats adjectives.
- One primary visitor on the first screen: the hiring founder or ops lead.
- The site itself should feel like a useful system, not a brochure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable and usable as the bar. Strong contrast, visible focus, and reduced-motion alternatives. No extra WCAG program beyond that.
