FAQ

Common questions about my work.

What kind of AI work does Yonatan do?

I turn AI ideas into useful steps for research, reports, drafts, checks, ecommerce, and team tasks. The goal is a process people can use, review, and improve.

Can Yonatan improve spreadsheets, dashboards, or reports?

Yes. I find which files matter, remove repeat work, clean key fields, and turn messy data into clear reports or dashboards.

Does Yonatan work with ecommerce businesses?

Yes. I have worked with Amazon, eBay, inventory, product data, listings, shipping, warehouses, ads, reports, and process fixes.

Can Yonatan build Custom GPTs or AI assistants?

Yes, when they are the right tool. I can build assistants, prompts, routing steps, and review checks. I fit them into the larger way your team works.

Does Yonatan offer tutoring or one-on-one technical help?

Yes. I can help individuals, students, founders, operators, and teams understand AI tools, troubleshoot workflows, choose software, build projects, improve SOPs, or get unstuck.

Is Yonatan available for remote work?

Yes. I am based in North Miami Beach, Florida. I am open to remote consulting, contract projects, and hands-on project help.
