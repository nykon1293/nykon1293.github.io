The problem

Uncategorized transactions pile up. The same vendors get coded differently.

What SI does

Using the firm's chart of accounts and past approved coding, SI drafts suggested categories. It flags unclear items as needs a question. It groups recurring vendors so the bookkeeper can approve them in bulk.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Bookkeeping firm with recurring clients

    Recurring vendors are grouped for bulk review. Unclear items are flagged needs a question.

  • Solo CPA

    Suggestions follow the firm's chart of accounts. Nothing is posted until the bookkeeper approves.

  • Two-partner accounting firm

    SI does not decide what is deductible or capitalizable.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Check each tool before it holds client information. Match it to the written information security plan. Confirm the agreements and any client consent needed for tax return information. Set access controls and multi-factor authentication. Limit SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use the firm's chart of accounts and past approved coding only. Permission is to suggest, not to post. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The CPA, EA, or preparer reviews and approves before anything goes to a client, agency, or third party.
  • SI never sends, files, e-signs, or posts on its own.
  • Unusual items go to a "needs professional review" queue.
  • The firm decides what client data SI may see. Testing starts with sample or made-up information.
  • The bookkeeper reviews and approves every category before it is posted. The accountant decides tax and accounting treatment.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using this chart of accounts and past approved coding [paste], draft suggested categories for these transactions [paste]. Flag unclear items as needs a question, and group recurring vendors for bulk review. Suggestions are drafts based on the firm's own rules. Do not post, decide what is deductible or capitalizable, or give tax treatment. Pause and show me before sending, filing, posting or changing anything. Use only the sample or made-up information I provide; do not include real client names, SSNs, account numbers or tax details.

Typical effect

Suggested categories wait for the bookkeeper. Unclear items are set aside as questions.
