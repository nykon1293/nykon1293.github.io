Content & marketing

One talk, class or article into several pieces

SI turns one long piece, such as a talk, webinar, interview or article, into shorter posts, an email, a handout and short video scripts.

The problem

Good long content gets used once. Making shorter versions for other channels takes as long as writing the original.

What SI does

From a recording or transcript, SI pulls out the strongest points and drafts several pieces: a summary article, an email, a few short social posts, a one-page handout and short video scripts with suggested clip timestamps. Each piece keeps your wording where possible and notes its source.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Employment law firm

    A webinar on new leave rules becomes a client alert, an email, three short posts and a one-page summary for HR managers, all reviewed by an attorney.

  • Fitness coach

    A long podcast episode becomes short exercise tips and a handout for clients.

  • Nonprofit

    The director's annual meeting speech becomes a donor letter and a series of impact posts.

What it takes to set up

The source recording or document and a style example. Setup is often a day.

What a human still checks

  • The speaker or author approves each piece.
  • Quotes are checked against the transcript.
  • Nothing is published without approval.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Here is a transcript of [talk/article]: [file]. Draft a summary article, an email, [5] social posts, a one-page handout and [3] short video scripts with timestamps for clips. Keep my wording where possible. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

More value from each piece of content, with less time spent rewriting it.
