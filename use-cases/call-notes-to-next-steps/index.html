Call notes into a follow-up email and tasks

After a sales or client call, SI turns your notes or the transcript into a recap email, CRM updates and dated tasks.

Worth knowing: Call recording: consent rules differ by state and country, and some require every party to agree. Tell participants and follow the stricter rule if unsure.

The problem

After a call, the follow-up email and the CRM update are the first things to slip. Details are forgotten, and promised next steps happen late or not at all.

What SI does

From typed notes, a voice memo or a call transcript, SI writes a short recap: what the client needs, what was agreed, open questions and who does what by when. It drafts the follow-up email, updates the CRM fields and creates tasks with due dates.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Marketing agency

    A discovery call transcript becomes a recap listing the client's goals, the two campaigns discussed and a promise to send a proposal by Thursday, with a task created for it.

  • Solar installer

    A rep's voice memo from a site visit becomes notes on roof orientation and the homeowner's financing question, plus a follow-up email with the requested information.

  • IT managed-services provider

    Notes from a call with a medical office about backups become a task list and an email confirming the next meeting.

What it takes to set up

A way to get notes or transcripts to SI, and access to the CRM and email drafts. Setup is often a day or two.

What a human still checks

  • The person who was on the call reviews the recap and email before sending.
  • Pricing or commitments in the recap are checked against what was actually said.
  • Recording only happens with the consent required where you operate.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Here are my notes or transcript from a call with [client]: [paste]. Write a recap with needs, decisions, open questions and next steps with owners and dates. Draft a follow-up email in my voice. List the CRM fields to update and tasks to create. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Follow-ups go out while the call is fresh, and the CRM reflects what was actually agreed.
