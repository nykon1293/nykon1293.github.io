The problem

A contract is signed, and the crew still waits on a work order someone has to type. An insurance form on file can name the wrong job or show old dates.

What SI does

SI reads the signed contract and drafts a work order with the job, the site, and the dates the contract states. It reads the insurance form the owner saved and flags it when the name, dates, or dollar amount do not match the job rules the owner wrote. The owner approves the work order and the flag before anyone is told. SI does not decide if coverage is enough. SI does not send the work order or tell a sub to start.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Roofing contractor

    A signed contract names the address and start week. SI drafts the work order. The owner sends it to the crew.

  • HVAC company

    The insurance form shows a date that already passed. SI flags it. The owner asks the sub for a new form before work is scheduled.

  • Remodel shop

    The form names a different company than the job rules. SI flags the name. The owner reviews it before anyone is told to start.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds customer information, the business checks the tool's terms. It also checks data practices. It sets access controls. It limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use a sample contract, the work-order layout, and the job rules the owner wrote for insurance forms. Permission is to draft, not to send. We sit with you, plan the steps, and teach you to run it. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The owner approves the work order before it goes to the crew or a sub.
  • The owner or office manager approves before anything goes to a customer, vendor, or inspector.
  • SI never sends a quote, takes a payment, books a job, or submits anything on its own.
  • SI does not decide if an insurance form is enough.
  • The business decides what customer data SI may see. Testing starts with sample information.
