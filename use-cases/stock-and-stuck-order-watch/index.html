The problem

Problems are found when a customer complains. An item sold that was not in stock. A shipment stuck for a week. A job waiting on a part nobody ordered.

What SI does

SI checks stock levels against minimums. It compares open customer orders or jobs with the stock and parts they need. It looks for orders with no movement beyond a set time. No shipping scan or no status change counts as no movement. It sends a short daily list. The list says what is wrong and a suggested next step. Examples are contacting the customer, transferring stock, or chasing a carrier. It can also flag orders that are still waiting to ship past the time you set. You decide the next step.

Related in this library: Morning watch for late, stuck, and returned packages, Heads-up delay emails when a shipment stalls.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Auto parts distributor

    Two customer orders depend on a part with zero stock and no open purchase order. The list suggests ordering and warning the customers.

  • Furniture retailer

    A delivery has shown no carrier update for six days. A draft message to the customer and a carrier inquiry are prepared.

  • Appliance repair company

    Three scheduled repairs need a part not yet received. The dispatcher can reschedule before the technician drives out.

  • Shopify store that promises two-day fulfillment

    Eight paid orders are unfulfilled at 30 hours, and two are overdue. The list is grouped by due time.

  • Marketplace seller

    Three orders hit ship-by tomorrow morning with no label. SI lists them with SKU and bin location if the sheet has it.

  • Multi-channel seller

    Marketplace orders and store orders sit in one list, tagged by channel. The warehouse works the tightest clock first.

What it takes to set up

Read access to stock and order or job data. Carrier tracking if relevant. Setup is often several days.

What a human still checks

  • A person decides on each fix. They approve any customer message.
  • Staff make stock changes in the system.
  • Thresholds are tuned to avoid false alarms.
  • A person decides the pick order and any customer message.
  • SI does not purchase postage or mark orders fulfilled.
  • Hold, fraud-review, and preorder orders are excluded if you tag them.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Each [morning], check stock in [system] against minimums [file], open orders or jobs against the stock or parts they need, and order tracking for anything with no update in [5] days. List problems with a suggested next step for each. Draft customer messages where needed. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Problems surface while there is still time to fix them quietly.
