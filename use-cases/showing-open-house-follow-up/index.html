Showing and open-house follow-up drafts

A sign-in sheet becomes a contact list, with the same thank-you and questions follow-up drafted from the agent's approved wording.

Admin and operations help for agents, landlords and small property managers. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner; they review and decide. SI does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Worth knowing: Administrative support only: SI does not give legal or lease advice, make leasing, screening or pricing decisions, or act as an agent or broker. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants. A licensed professional or the property owner reviews and approves anything client-facing, tenant-facing or contractual. Fair housing laws apply: SI never screens, ranks, steers or targets by protected characteristics, and applicant and tenant information is used only in checked tools with access controls and the minimum necessary. Every visitor gets the same follow-up; SI never tailors messages to a person's apparent race, family, religion, disability or other protected trait, and never makes statements about neighbors, schools or type of residents. Texting rules vary; get consent and honor opt-outs.

The problem

Visitors sign in and then hear nothing, so interest cools.

What SI does

SI turns a sign-in sheet or visit list into a tidy contact list, drafts a thank-you note and a questions follow-up for every visitor from the agent's approved wording, and logs who responded. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Independent real estate agent

    An open-house sign-in list becomes a contact sheet. Each visitor gets the same thank-you draft from the agent's wording.

  • Small brokerage

    A showing visit list is logged with who responded. Follow-up drafts do not mention neighbors, schools, or who would fit.

  • Small property-management company

    Everyone who toured [Unit] is sent the same approved questions follow-up after the manager reviews it.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds applicant or tenant information, the business checks the tool's terms and data practices, sets access controls and limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Start from a sample sign-in sheet and the follow-up wording the agent has already approved. Permission is to draft, not to send. Setup is often a day or two.

What a human still checks

  • The agent, broker or manager approves before anything goes to a client, tenant, applicant or vendor.
  • SI never sends, posts, signs or changes records on its own.
  • Unusual items go to a "needs manager or attorney review" queue.
  • The business decides what personal data SI may see, and testing starts with sample information.
  • The agent approves the message, decides who to call personally, and answers all questions about the property and terms.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Turn this sign-in sheet or visit list [paste] into a contact list. Draft a thank-you and a questions follow-up for every visitor from this approved wording only: [paste]. Give every visitor the same follow-up. Do not tailor messages to a person, and do not mention neighbors, schools, or type of residents. Do not send anything. Texting rules vary; get consent and honor opt-outs. Pause and show me before sending, posting, signing or changing anything. Use only the sample or made-up information I provide; do not include real tenant or applicant names, IDs or financial details.

Typical effect

Every visitor is on one list with the same follow-up draft, ready for the agent to send.
