Matching receipts to card transactions

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) matches receipts from email and photos to card transactions. It suggests categories. It lists what is still missing before month end.

The problem

Card transactions pile up without receipts. At month end someone hunts through email and phone photos to match them. Expense reports are late.

What SI does

SI reads receipts from a forwarding inbox or shared folder. It matches them to transactions by amount, date, and merchant. It suggests a category from past coding. It builds a missing-receipts list per cardholder. It can also assemble a simple expense report for staff who pay out of pocket.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Electrical contractor

    Fuel and hardware-store receipts snapped by field crews are matched to the company cards. Each crew lead gets a short list of what is missing.

  • Consulting firm

    Travel receipts from a client trip are matched. They are grouped into an expense report ready to be billed back to the client.

  • Nonprofit

    Program staff purchases are matched and coded to the right grant. Grant reports are easier at quarter end.

What it takes to set up

Card transaction feed or export. A receipts inbox or folder. The chart of accounts. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The bookkeeper confirms categories before anything is posted.
  • Reminders to cardholders about missing receipts are reviewed before sending.
  • Unusual or personal-looking charges are flagged for the owner.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Match these card transactions [export] to the receipts in [folder/inbox]. Match on amount, date (within [3] days) and merchant. Suggest a category using this past coding [file]. List unmatched transactions by cardholder, and flag anything unusual. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Month end has fewer surprises. Chasing receipts becomes a short list instead of a search.
