Customer support

After-hours and busy-time phone coverage

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. A voice assistant answers calls you would miss. It handles common questions. It takes detailed messages or requests. Then it sends staff a summary.

Worth knowing: Call recording and automated calls are regulated. Consent rules differ by state and country. Disclose the assistant and any recording. Check the rules that apply before going live. Medical practices should keep the assistant away from clinical questions.

The problem

Calls that go to voicemail often never call back. Small offices cannot staff the phone through lunch, evenings, and weekends. Busy moments mean the person on the phone cannot also serve the person in front of them.

What SI does

When nobody picks up, a voice assistant answers with your greeting. It handles a short list of approved questions. Those include hours, location, services, and how to book. It takes a structured message: name, number, reason, urgency. For true emergencies it follows your escalation rule. That can mean forwarding to the on-call phone. Staff get a text or email summary of each call.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Heating and cooling contractor

    A 9 p.m. no-heat call is identified as urgent. It is forwarded to the on-call technician. A request for a maintenance quote becomes a message for the morning.

  • Small medical practice

    Over lunch the assistant gives office hours and directions. It takes callback messages. Anything that sounds urgent goes to the instructions you approved.

  • Towing and roadside service

    After hours, callers' location and vehicle details are collected. They are texted to the driver on duty.

What it takes to set up

A phone number or forwarding rule. The approved question list. Your escalation rules. A message format. Setup is often one to two weeks. That includes test calls.

What a human still checks

  • Callers are told they are speaking with an automated assistant.
  • Emergency rules are tested before going live. They are reviewed regularly.
  • Staff return every message. The assistant does not make commitments on price or timing beyond what you approved.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Draft a phone script for after-hours calls to [business]. It should greet callers, say it is an automated assistant, answer only these questions [list], take a message with name, number, reason and urgency, and treat these situations as emergencies [list] by [forwarding to number]. Write the summary text staff will receive. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Fewer missed calls turn into lost customers. Staff start the day with clear messages instead of voicemails.
