Lease renewal and end-date tracking

Lease dates the manager has verified sit in one tracker. SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) adds reminders and a neutral draft for the manager to review.

The problem

Lease end dates sneak up. Renewals, rent reviews, and turnover planning start too late.

What SI does

SI builds a tracker from the lease dates the manager enters. That includes end date, notice windows the manager has verified, and renewal status. SI reminds the manager at intervals they set. SI drafts a neutral your-lease-ends-on-[date], let's-talk message for review. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner. SI does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Small property-management company

    End dates the manager entered for each unit sit in one tracker. Reminders use the intervals the manager set.

  • Landlord with a few rental units

    A draft for [Unit] says the lease ends on the date the owner entered. It asks to talk. The owner reviews it before it is sent.

  • Independent real estate agent

    A weekly list shows which leases the agent marked as coming up. It is drawn only from dates someone has already verified.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds applicant or tenant information, the business checks the tool's terms. It also checks data practices. It sets access controls. It limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use dates a person has already verified against the lease. Only those dates may be entered. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The agent, broker, or manager approves before anything goes to a client, tenant, applicant, or vendor.
  • SI never sends, posts, signs, or changes records on its own.
  • Unusual items go to a "needs manager or attorney review" queue.
  • The business decides what personal data SI may see. Testing starts with sample information.
  • The manager verifies dates and notice rules against the actual lease and the law. The manager decides renewal terms and rent. The manager sends everything.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Build a tracker from these lease dates a person has already verified [paste], including end date, notice windows the manager verified, and renewal status. Remind at these intervals [paste]. Draft a neutral message that the lease ends on [date] and that they should talk. Do not calculate legal notice deadlines, propose rent amounts or renewal terms, or decide who is renewed. Do not send anything. Pause and show me before sending, posting, signing or changing anything. Use only the sample or made-up information I provide; do not include real tenant or applicant names, IDs or financial details.

Typical effect

Verified end dates sit in one list with reminders. The renewal draft waits for the manager.
