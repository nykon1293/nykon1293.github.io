Volunteer sign-ups, reminders and gap filling

SI organizes volunteer sign-ups, sends reminders, spots unfilled shifts, and drafts targeted asks to people likely to help.

The problem

Volunteer coordination runs on group texts and memory. Shifts go unfilled until the last minute, and the same few people get asked every time.

What SI does

SI keeps the sign-up sheet current from form responses and replies, sends reminders before each shift, and lists unfilled slots. For gaps, it drafts a short ask to volunteers who have helped with that kind of task before but have not been asked recently.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Soup kitchen

    Weekday lunch shifts are tracked, and a Wednesday gap gets an ask to three people who have done that shift before.

  • Habitat-style home building nonprofit

    Build-day volunteers get reminders with the site address and what to wear.

  • Elementary school PTA

    Book-fair shifts are filled and parents get reminders the evening before.

What it takes to set up

A sign-up form or sheet, volunteer contacts and past participation. Setup is often a day or two.

What a human still checks

  • The coordinator approves messages to volunteers.
  • Volunteers who opted out are not contacted.
  • Roles that need background checks are filled only by cleared volunteers.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using sign-ups [sheet/form] and volunteer history [file], list unfilled shifts for [period]. Draft reminders for confirmed volunteers [1] day before. For each gap, draft a short ask to [3] volunteers who have done similar shifts and have not been asked in [30] days. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Fewer last-minute gaps, and the volunteer load is spread more evenly.
