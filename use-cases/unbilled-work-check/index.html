Finding work that was done but never invoiced

SI compares completed jobs, time entries or deliveries against invoices sent and lists anything that was never billed.

The problem

Small jobs, extra hours and change orders get done and then forgotten at billing time. Nobody notices the gap because nothing looks wrong.

What SI does

SI lines up the record of work done (closed job tickets, timesheets, delivery notes, change orders) against invoices issued. It lists items with no matching invoice, partial billing, or hours logged above what was billed, and prepares draft invoices or a list for the billing person.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Plumbing company

    A closed ticket for a water heater flush and two change orders on a bathroom job have no invoice; drafts are prepared for the office manager.

  • Law firm

    Logged hours on a matter exceed what was billed last month, and the gap is listed for the billing partner to review.

  • Equipment rental company

    Rental extensions recorded at return were never added to the customer's invoice.

What it takes to set up

Read access to the job, time or delivery system and to invoicing. Setup is often a few days, mostly agreeing on how to match records.

What a human still checks

  • The owner or billing person confirms each item before any invoice is drafted or sent.
  • Work done as a courtesy or under warranty is marked so it is not flagged again.
  • Customer-facing invoices are approved before sending.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Compare these completed jobs or time entries [export] with these invoices [export] for [period]. Match on [job number, client, date]. List work with no invoice, partial invoices, and hours logged above hours billed. Draft invoices only for items I confirm. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Less revenue slips away unnoticed, and billing reviews have something concrete to check.
