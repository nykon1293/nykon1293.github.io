The problem

Certificates of insurance, licenses, and W-9s for subs and vendors expire. They go missing until a customer or GC asks.

What SI does

SI keeps a tracker of the business's own and its subs' license, insurance, and tax-form documents. It also tracks dates the owner has verified. SI reminds the office ahead of expiry. SI drafts please-send-an-updated-certificate requests.

Related in this library: Licenses, insurance, and certification tracking, Permit and inspection tracking, Quote and estimate drafting.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Two-crew roofing business

    Expiry dates the owner verified sit in one tracker. A request draft for an updated certificate waits for the owner.

  • Small HVAC company

    The office is reminded ahead of a date the owner entered. SI does not confirm that a policy is valid.

  • Landscaping crew

    W-9 and license documents are listed as on file or missing. The owner decides who may work.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds customer information, the business checks the tool's terms. It also checks data practices. It sets access controls. It limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use dates the owner has verified against the issuing source. Permission is to track and draft, not to confirm validity. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The owner verifies documents against the issuing source. The owner decides whether a sub may work. The owner handles renewals.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Keep a tracker of these license, insurance, and tax-form documents and dates the owner has verified [paste]. Remind the office ahead of expiry. Draft please-send-an-updated-certificate requests from this wording [paste]. Do not confirm that a license or policy is valid, decide who may do licensed work, or give insurance or licensing advice. Do not send anything. Pause and show me before sending, quoting, booking, ordering or changing anything. Use only the sample or made-up information I provide; do not include real customer names, addresses or payment details.

Typical effect

Document dates the owner verified are visible. Request drafts are ready before something expires.
