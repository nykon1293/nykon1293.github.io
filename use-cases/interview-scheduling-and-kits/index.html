Interview scheduling and a consistent question kit

SI coordinates interview times with candidates and interviewers, and prepares a consistent question set and scoring sheet for each role.

Worth knowing: Hiring: employment and anti-discrimination laws apply. Screening criteria must be job-related and applied consistently, SI should never score on protected characteristics, and some places regulate automated hiring tools. A person makes every hiring decision.

The problem

Interviews take a string of emails to schedule. Each interviewer asks different questions, which makes candidates hard to compare.

What SI does

SI drafts messages to shortlisted candidates offering times that work for the interviewers, books confirmed slots, and sends reminders. For each role it prepares a short kit: the same job-related questions for every candidate, what a good answer covers, and a simple scoring sheet for interviewers to fill in.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Dental practice

    Three hygienist candidates are offered times around the dentist's patient schedule, and the kit covers patient communication and sterilization procedures.

  • Marketing agency

    A designer candidate gets times with two interviewers and a short portfolio review structure.

  • Nonprofit

    Program coordinator interviews use the same questions for all candidates, including board members on the panel.

What it takes to set up

Calendar access for interviewers, candidate contacts and the role's criteria. Setup is often a day.

What a human still checks

  • Candidate messages are approved before sending.
  • The hiring manager approves the questions.
  • Interview notes and scores are kept with access limited to the hiring team.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
For [role], draft messages to these candidates [list] offering [3] times that fit [interviewers' calendars]. Prepare an interview kit with [6] job-related questions, what a strong answer covers, and a scoring sheet. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Less scheduling back-and-forth and fairer, easier comparisons between candidates.
