Membership renewals and a clean member list

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) tracks renewal dates and drafts reminders. It cleans up the member list. It merges duplicates and flags outdated contact details.

The problem

Renewals depend on someone remembering to send reminders. The member list has duplicates, old emails, and households listed three ways.

What SI does

SI reads the member list and payment records. It lists members due to renew or lapsed. It drafts reminders in a friendly sequence. It proposes cleanup. That includes merging duplicates, flagging bounced emails, and making names and households consistent. Lapsed members who were active get a personal-style note. A board member can send it.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Neighborhood swim club

    Families due to renew in March get reminders. Households listed under two parents' names are merged.

  • Trade association

    Lapsed member companies are listed with their last event attended. A board member can call them.

  • Garden club

    Bounced email addresses are flagged ahead of the spring renewal mailing.

What it takes to set up

Member list and payment exports, and your renewal schedule. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • Merges and deletions are confirmed by a person.
  • Reminders are approved before sending.
  • Members with hardship arrangements are handled personally.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using the member list [file] and payments [file], list members due to renew in [period] and those lapsed. Draft a reminder sequence [timing]. Propose merges for likely duplicates, flag bounced or missing emails, and standardize household names. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

More renewals happen on time. The member list is one you can trust.
