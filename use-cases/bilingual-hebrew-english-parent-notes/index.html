Bilingual Hebrew and English parent notes from approved wording

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) prepares a Hebrew and English parent note from wording staff already approved, and the note waits in a queue until after Shabbat or Yom Tov.

Admin help for teachers, tutors and schools. SI drafts and organizes; a person approves anything sent to a student or parent. No grading decisions, and student records stay on the school's own equipment and accounts.

Worth knowing: Administrative support only: SI does not grade, evaluate, or make academic decisions, and does not replace a teacher's or school's judgment. A teacher, tutor, or school staff member reviews and approves anything sent to a student or parent. Student records are used only on the school's own equipment and accounts, with access controls and the minimum necessary, never in consumer tools. SI fills staff-approved wording only. It does not translate religious texts or write Judaic lesson content. Nothing sends, posts, or runs on Shabbat or Yom Tov; drafts wait in a queue until after. No images of people. FERPA still applies to any student names in a note.

The problem

The office often needs the same parent note in Hebrew and in English. Drafting both by hand is slow, and a message must not go out on Shabbat or Yom Tov.

What SI does

SI fills the school's approved Hebrew and English templates from the facts staff already wrote (date, time, room, what to bring). A staff member who can read both languages reviews the draft. The message is held in a queue until after Shabbat or Yom Tov. SI does not compose religious content, does not write religious rulings or Judaic lesson content, and does not create or attach images of people.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school

    A dismissal-time change the office already decided is filled into the approved Hebrew and English templates and held until after Shabbat.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school office

    A reminder to return a form uses only the school's saved sentences in both languages. Staff review the Hebrew before anyone is sent the note.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school grade office

    The draft names only the date and room already written by staff. No photograph is attached.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds student records, the school reviews the vendor's terms, access controls, and any FERPA or state student-privacy duties. Start with sample or de-identified material. The school's approved Hebrew and English note templates and a short list of facts for one notice. Permission is to draft, not to send. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • A staff member who can read the Hebrew and the English approves the note before a student or parent receives it.
  • Nothing sends, posts, or runs on Shabbat or Yom Tov; approved messages wait in a queue until after.
  • SI does not create or attach images of people.
  • SI does not write religious rulings or Judaic lesson content.

Try it yourself

Fill this approved Hebrew template [paste] and this approved English template [paste] using only these facts staff already wrote [paste]. Do not add sentences that are not in the templates.
