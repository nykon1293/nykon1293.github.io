What SI can take off your plate

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can take on a lot of repetitive work. It can read, sort, match, draft, check, and summarize. That work shows up in almost any business or group. Each entry describes one practical job. It shows what you need to start. It shows where a person stays in charge. Anything that sends, pays, orders, or posts always waits for your approval.

The examples show how the same job looks in different kinds of businesses. They are examples of what is possible. They are not reports of client results. Setup times are rough. They depend on how clean your data and tools are.
