What SI can take off your plate

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can take on a lot of the repetitive work in almost any business or organization: reading, sorting, matching, drafting, checking and summarizing. Each entry describes one practical job, what it needs to get started, and where a person stays in charge. Anything that sends, pays, orders or posts always waits for your approval.

The examples show how the same job looks in different kinds of businesses. They are illustrations of what is possible, not reports of client results. Setup times are rough and depend on how clean your data and tools are.
