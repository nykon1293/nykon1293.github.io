Tracking staff licenses, certifications and training renewals

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) keeps one staff credential list. It covers licenses, certifications, and required training. The list includes expiry dates. It reminds people well before anything lapses.

The problem

Licenses, certifications, and required trainings expire on different dates. A lapse can mean someone cannot work. It can also mean a problem at an inspection or audit.

What SI does

SI reads certificates and records from a shared folder or HR system. It builds a list of each person's credentials. The list includes issue and expiry dates. It sends reminders to the person and their manager ahead of each expiry. It flags anyone scheduled for work that needs a credential they do not hold.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Electrical contractor

    Electrician licenses, lift certifications, and safety training are tracked. A reminder goes out to renew a lift card before a job that needs it.

  • Childcare center

    CPR and first-aid certifications are tracked for every staff member. Background-check renewals are tracked too.

  • Home health agency

    Nursing licenses are tracked for each caregiver. Required annual trainings are tracked too.

What it takes to set up

Copies of certificates or HR records. You also need the list of required credentials per role. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • A manager confirms dates against the original documents.
  • A person makes scheduling decisions about uncertified staff.
  • Credential records are kept with access limited to managers.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
From these certificates and records [folder], build a list of each person's licenses, certifications and trainings with issue and expiry dates. Compare with required credentials per role [file]. Draft reminders [60] and [14] days before expiry, and flag anyone scheduled for work needing a credential they lack. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Fewer surprise lapses. Fewer scrambles before inspections.
