Tuition and payment reminder drafts for the business office

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) drafts polite tuition reminders from the aging list the business office already keeps. A person in that office approves every message.

Book a free 30-minute consult

Worth knowing: SI drafts from the office's own list. It does not collect money or give financial advice.

Want this built? Book a free 30-minute call

The problem

Tuition reminders are overdue work for a small business office. Wording has to stay calm and accurate, and accounts that need a personal call get mixed in with routine reminders.

What SI does

SI reads the aging list the office exports and drafts a reminder from the school's approved wording at the times the office already set. It highlights accounts the office marked for a personal call or a payment-plan conversation. It does not take payment, apply fees, or change balances.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Independent school business office

    The aging list becomes draft reminders at the school's usual times. Rows the office tagged call are set aside.

  • Small private elementary school

    A draft names only the amount and due date already on the statement. It uses approved wording.

  • Tutoring studio with monthly invoices

    Open invoices from the export get a short draft. The owner sends it.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. The school reviews each tool before it holds student records. Check the vendor's terms, access controls, and any FERPA or state student-privacy duties. Start with sample or de-identified material. Use an aging export. Use the reminder wording and times the business office has already approved. Permission is to read and draft, not to send or charge. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The business office reviews and sends every reminder.
  • A person approves anything sent to a student or parent.
  • SI never takes payment, adds a fee, or changes a balance.
  • Accounts marked sensitive go to a person without a draft.
  • SI never sends, posts, or changes a record on its own.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using this aging export [file] and these approved reminder intervals and wording [paste], draft reminders for open balances. Set aside accounts marked for a personal call. Do not take payment, add fees, or change balances. Pause and show me before sending, posting or changing anything. Use only the sample or de-identified information I provide; do not include real student names, grades, or family contact details.

Typical effect

Routine tuition drafts wait for the business office. Sensitive accounts stay with a person.
