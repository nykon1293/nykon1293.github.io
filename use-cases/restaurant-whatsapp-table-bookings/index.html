The problem

Booking chats arrive while the host is on the floor. Tables get double booked. Guests wait for a yes that never comes.

What SI does

SI reads the open times and the guest's message. It drafts a reply that offers a table, a class, a court, or a tutoring time. It does not hold the slot or take a deposit. A restaurant, a gym, and a tutor can use the same pattern. A person approves anything sent to a student or parent. This is not medical advice.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Restaurant

    A WhatsApp note asks for four people at 7. SI drafts a reply from open tables. The host sends it.

  • Tutor

    A parent asks for a Thursday session on WhatsApp. SI drafts a time from the tutor's sheet. The tutor sends it.

  • Gym or court

    A chat asks to book a court. SI drafts a reply from the open grid. Staff confirm.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Connect the chat you already use and the calendar or sheet of open times. Add two replies you like. Setup is often a day or two.
