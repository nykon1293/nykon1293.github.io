The problem

Who was called lives in texts and memory. So do the quote and whether the job got done.

What SI does

SI keeps a vendor list with trade, contact, rates the manager entered, and notes. SI also keeps a work-order tracker with ticket, vendor, quote, status, and invoice received. SI drafts scheduling and status messages. SI lists open and stale jobs each week. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner. SI does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Related in this library: Maintenance request intake and sorting, Landlord morning list of inquiries, showings, rent, and repairs, Work requests into scheduled jobs and work orders.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Small property-management company

    Open work orders are listed by status. A status draft to a vendor waits for the manager to send it.

  • Landlord with a few rental units

    The vendor list holds trades and contacts the owner entered. SI does not recommend who to hire.

  • Independent real estate agent

    A weekly list shows stale jobs on properties the agent manages. The owner can follow up.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds applicant or tenant information, the business checks the tool's terms. It also checks data practices. It sets access controls. It limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Start from the vendor list and work-order sheet the manager already keeps. Permission is to draft, not to hire or pay. Setup is often a few days.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Update this vendor list and work-order tracker [paste] with these items [paste]. Draft scheduling and status messages from this approved wording [paste]. List open and stale jobs. Do not hire, approve spending, or pay. Do not recommend a vendor on quality or safety grounds, and do not vouch that a vendor is licensed or insured. Give no repair or code advice. Do not send anything. Pause and show me before sending, posting, signing or changing anything. Use only the sample or made-up information I provide; do not include real tenant or applicant names, IDs or financial details.
