Holiday-hours updates across listings

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) compares the holiday or season hours you actually run with each place those hours appear. It drafts the corrections for you to post.

The problem

Holiday and season hours live in too many places: Google, Apple Maps, Facebook, the website, and the reservation book. Google Business Profile upkeep checks one listing. It does not line up the same hours across every place customers look.

What SI does

SI takes the hours you treat as current for the dates you name. It compares them with the copy you export or paste from each listing. It lists what is stale. It drafts the corrected hours, a short closure note, and a checklist of places still not updated. It does not publish anything.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Neighborhood restaurant

    Thanksgiving hours are closed on Google but still a normal dinner service on Facebook and the website. SI drafts the matching closure text for each place.

  • Farm stand

    Peak-season Saturday hours were extended in the reservation sheet. They were never copied to Apple Maps or the hours page.

  • Hair salon

    December 24 is a short day in the appointment book. SI lists Google, Instagram, and the booking site as still showing a full day. It drafts hours for each.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. You need the hours you want shown for the holiday or season. You also need a copy of each listing (Google, Apple, Facebook, website, reservation tool). Access stays with the person who posts. Setup is often a day, then a repeating check before each holiday.

What a human still checks

  • A person confirms the real hours before anything is posted.
  • A person approves anything that sends, pays, orders, or posts.
  • SI does not invent reviews, ratings, or claims about rankings.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Here are the hours we actually run for [dates] [paste]. Compare them with these listing copies [Google, Apple, Facebook, website, reservation tool]. List what is stale and draft the corrected hours and a short note for each place. Do not invent reviews or ranking claims. Pause and show me before sending, paying, ordering, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

Holiday and season hours are drafted once from the real calendar. A person still posts each listing.
