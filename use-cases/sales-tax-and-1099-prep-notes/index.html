Sales-tax and 1099 prep notes for the accountant

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) gathers store tax reports, marketplace tax lines, and contractor payments. It builds a notes packet for your accountant. It does not file anything.

Book a free 30-minute consult

Worth knowing: SI organizes reports and drafts notes. It does not give tax advice, decide nexus, or file returns. A qualified accountant or tax preparer makes those calls.

The problem

The store, the marketplace, and the payment processor each show tax a different way. Sales-tax returns and 1099 forms are the accountant's job, but the store owner still has to find the reports. A general year-end packet gathers folders. It does not line up ecommerce tax reports with what a marketplace already remitted.

What SI does

SI collects the reports you point it at. That includes the store tax summary and marketplace tax or facilitator statements. It also includes payout reports and contractor payment totals. It builds a notes sheet. The sheet shows tax collected vs remitted by channel. It shows jurisdictions named in the reports and 1099-K figures as they appear. It shows contractor totals that may need a 1099-NEC. It lists gaps, such as a channel with no report or a missing month. It does not decide nexus, what is taxable, or what to file.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Shopify store selling in several states

    Notes show tax collected in the store vs tax a marketplace says it remitted as facilitator. Months that have no marketplace file are listed as missing.

  • Multi-channel seller

    1099-K figures from the processor are copied as printed, next to payout totals, for the accountant to reconcile.

  • Brand that pays makers

    Contractor payments from the year are totaled by person from the bill sheet. A note says addresses still need to be confirmed before any 1099-NEC.

What it takes to set up

You need the year's store and marketplace tax reports. You need payout exports and a list of contractors you paid. Last year's filing or the accountant's checklist helps if you have it. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The accountant decides what to file. SI makes no tax judgments.
  • Totals are checked against the source reports.
  • Sensitive documents go only through the accountant's channel.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using these store tax reports, marketplace tax or facilitator statements, payout exports, and contractor payments [files], build a notes sheet for [year]: tax collected vs remitted by channel, jurisdictions named in the reports, 1099-K figures as printed, and contractor totals. List missing months or channels. Do not decide nexus, what is taxable, or what to file. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, filing, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

The accountant gets ecommerce-specific reports in one place. That is better than a folder of unlabeled exports.
