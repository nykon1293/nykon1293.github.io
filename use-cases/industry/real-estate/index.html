Use cases for real estate and property management

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Business-side admin for agents, landlords and small property managers. Licensed professionals and owners review and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the business side of real estate and property management: lead follow-up, scheduling, maintenance coordination, reminders, tracking and communications drafts. SI does not give legal, lease or financial advice, does not negotiate or sign anything, and does not replace the judgment of a licensed agent, broker or property manager. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants. A licensed professional or the property owner reviews and approves anything client-facing, tenant-facing or contractual before it goes out, and nothing here promises any sale, lease, price or result. Fair housing laws apply however a task is done: SI never screens, ranks, steers, targets or describes people or neighborhoods by protected characteristics, and every listing, ad and message is reviewed by a person before it is used. Applicant and tenant information is private, so it should only go into tools the business has checked, with access controls and the minimum necessary. Texting is regulated, so get consent and honor opt-outs. Licensing, landlord-tenant and advertising rules differ by state, so check yours. These pages are general information, not legal or compliance advice, and not a guarantee of compliance with any law.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Questions about Real estate SI (AI) use cases

No. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner. It does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants, and it does not give legal or lease advice.

Who approves listings and tenant messages?

A licensed professional or the property owner. Fair housing laws still apply. SI never screens, ranks, or describes people or neighborhoods by protected characteristics.

Does real estate SI run on my own computer?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send messages or change records on its own?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.
