Use cases for real estate and rentals

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Office help for agents, landlords, hosts, and appraisers. A person reviews every draft.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the business side of real estate and property management. That means lead follow-up, scheduling, maintenance coordination, reminders, tracking, and communications drafts. SI does not give legal, lease, or money advice. SI does not negotiate or sign anything. SI does not replace the judgment of a licensed agent, broker, or property manager. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants. A licensed professional or the property owner reviews and approves anything that faces a client, a tenant, or a contract before it goes out. Nothing here promises any sale, lease, price, or result. Fair housing laws apply however a task is done. SI never screens, ranks, steers, targets, or describes people or neighborhoods by protected characteristics. A person reviews every listing, ad, and message before it is used. Applicant and tenant information is private. It should only go into tools the business has checked, with access controls and the minimum necessary. Texting is regulated, so get consent and honor opt-outs. Licensing, landlord-tenant, and advertising rules differ by state, so check yours. These pages are general information. They are not legal or compliance advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with any law.
