Questions about Local & seasonal SI (AI) use cases

Will SI post holiday hours or send weather notices?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval. SI can draft the update; you post or send it.

Does local & seasonal SI run on my own computer?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send messages or change records on its own?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.
