Use cases for legal practices

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Office help for solo attorneys and small firms. Attorneys review and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the business side of a law practice. That means scheduling, intake logistics, billing admin, document organization, and communications drafts. SI does not give legal advice. SI does not practice law. SI does not replace an attorney's judgment. An attorney reviews and approves anything that faces a client or is substantive before it goes out. Nothing here promises any legal outcome or result. Client confidentiality comes first. Confidential or privileged client information should only go into a tool after the firm has checked the vendor's terms and data practices. The firm also needs proper agreements and access controls, plus any consent the rules require. ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024) addresses lawyers' use of generative artificial intelligence tools and is a good starting point. State bar rules differ, so check yours. Trust-account (IOLTA) and billing records are handled with extra care. A person approves every entry. These pages are general information. They are not legal or ethics advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with any rule.

Questions about Legal SI (AI) use cases

Does SI give legal advice?

No. These pages are office help only. SI does not give legal advice. SI does not practice law. SI does not replace an attorney's judgment. An attorney reviews anything that faces a client or is substantive.

Can we put client files in consumer chat tools?

Not by default. Confidential or privileged files go into a tool only after the firm checks terms. The firm also needs agreements, access controls, and any required consent. Start with sample data. Do not use real client names.

Does legal SI run on my own computer?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send messages or change records on its own?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.

Are these client results?

No. These are examples of what is possible. They are not reports of client work. Setup times are rough. They depend on how clean your data and tools are.
