Use cases for home services and contractors

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Office help for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, pool, and cleaning businesses. The owner approves every quote and message.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the office side of a home-service business. That means missed calls, quotes, scheduling, invoices, reminders, paperwork, and records. SI does not set prices. SI does not make promises. SI does not give safety, repair, code, or insurance advice. SI does not replace a licensed professional's judgment on the job. The owner or an authorized person reviews and approves every quote, price, schedule commitment, and customer message before it goes out. Nothing here promises any job, timeline, savings, or result. Licensing, advertising, deposit, permit, and contract rules differ by state and city, so check yours. The business's license number and required notices come from the owner, not from SI. Texting customers is regulated, so get consent and honor opt-outs. Customer information is private. It should only go into tools the business has checked. If something sounds like an emergency, such as gas, fire, smoke, or flooding near electricity, it goes to a person and to 911 or the utility. It never goes to SI. These pages are general information. They are not legal, safety, or compliance advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with any rule.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.
