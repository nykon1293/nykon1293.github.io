Use cases for accounting and tax firms

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Office help for small CPA, tax, and bookkeeping firms. Professionals review, sign, and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the practice side of an accounting, tax, or bookkeeping firm. That means chasing documents, organizing intake, tracking dates, preparing checklists, drafting routine notes, and getting information ready for a professional's review. SI does not give tax, accounting, or legal advice. SI does not prepare, sign, or file any return or report. SI does not replace a CPA's, enrolled agent's, or preparer's judgment. A qualified professional reviews, signs, and takes responsibility for all work product. Every deadline SI tracks is verified by a person against official sources. Nothing here promises any refund, tax result, or savings. Client financial and tax information is among the most sensitive a firm holds. Firms that prepare returns have legal duties around data security (the FTC Safeguards Rule, covered in IRS Publication 4557). They also have duties around using or disclosing tax return information (Internal Revenue Code section 7216). Professional rules apply on top. Put client information only into tools the firm has vetted. Use the agreements, access controls, and any client consent the rules require. Start with sample data. State rules differ, so check yours. These pages are general information. They are not tax, legal, or compliance advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with any rule.
