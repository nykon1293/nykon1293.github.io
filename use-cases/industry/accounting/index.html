Use cases for accounting and tax firms

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Admin for small CPA, tax and bookkeeping firms. Professionals review, sign and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the practice side of an accounting, tax or bookkeeping firm: chasing documents, organizing intake, tracking dates, preparing checklists, drafting routine communications and getting information ready for a professional's review. SI does not give tax, accounting or legal advice, does not prepare, sign or file any return or report, and does not replace a CPA's, enrolled agent's or preparer's judgment. A qualified professional reviews, signs and takes responsibility for all work product, and every deadline SI tracks is verified by a person against official sources. Nothing here promises any refund, tax result or savings. Client financial and tax information is among the most sensitive a firm holds: firms that prepare returns have legal duties around data security (the FTC Safeguards Rule, covered in IRS Publication 4557) and around using or disclosing tax return information (Internal Revenue Code section 7216), and professional rules apply on top. Put client information only into tools the firm has vetted, with the agreements, access controls and any client consent the rules require, and start with sample data. State rules differ, so check yours. These pages are general information, not tax, legal or compliance advice, and not a guarantee of compliance with any rule.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.
