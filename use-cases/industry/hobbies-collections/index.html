Questions about Hobbies & collections SI (AI) use cases

Is this only for sports cards?

No. Sports cards and TCG. Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, and other non-sports too. Sports cards go on SportsCardsPro. TCG stays on PriceCharting.

Does SI type my cards into PriceCharting for me?

You send a photo or say the card. SI drafts the add, the edit, the review, and the report. Sports cards go on SportsCardsPro. TCG and other non-sports stay on PriceCharting. You approve before anything is saved.

Does hobbies & collections SI run on my own computer?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send messages or change records on its own?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.

Are these client results?

No. These are examples of what is possible. They are not reports of client work. Setup times are rough. They depend on how clean your data and tools are.
