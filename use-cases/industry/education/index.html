Use cases for teachers, tutors and schools

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Admin for teachers, tutors and schools. A person approves anything sent to a student or parent.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the admin side of teaching and school work: parent notes, report-card comment drafts, outlines from the teacher's own standards, reminders, enrollment follow-up, substitute packets, form chasing, tuition drafts, and conference scheduling. SI does not grade, evaluate, promote, or discipline students, does not replace a teacher's or school's judgment, and does not give educational, legal or compliance advice. A teacher, tutor, or school staff member reviews and approves anything sent to a student or parent before it goes out, and nothing here promises any grade, admission, or result. Student privacy comes first: education records should only go into tools the school has checked, with access controls and the minimum necessary, never into consumer chat apps. FERPA and state student-privacy rules differ, so check yours. These pages are general information, not educational, legal or compliance advice, and not a guarantee of compliance with FERPA or any other rule. U.S. Department of Education FERPA guidance is a starting point. A clearly marked optional group below is for Orthodox Jewish day-school admin only: nothing sends, posts, or runs on Shabbat or Yom Tov; drafts wait in a queue until after; there are no images of people, no humor, and no religious rulings or Judaic teaching.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Education records belong only in tools the school has checked, with access controls and the minimum necessary, never in consumer chat apps. This is not a guarantee of FERPA compliance.

Does education SI run on my own computer?

Yes. GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.

Will SI send messages or change records on its own?

No. Anything that sends, pays, orders, posts, or changes a record waits for your approval.

No. They are illustrations of what is possible, not reports of client work. Setup times are rough and depend on how clean your data and tools are.
