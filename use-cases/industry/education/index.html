Use cases for teachers, tutors and schools

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Office help for teachers, tutors, and schools. A person approves anything sent to a student or parent.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the admin side of teaching and school work. That means parent notes, report-card comment drafts, outlines from the teacher's own standards, reminders, enrollment follow-up, substitute packets, form chasing, tuition drafts, and conference scheduling. SI does not grade, evaluate, promote, or discipline students. SI does not replace a teacher's or school's judgment. SI does not give educational, legal, or compliance advice. A teacher, tutor, or school staff member reviews and approves anything sent to a student or parent before it goes out. Nothing here promises any grade, admission, or result. Student privacy comes first. Education records should only go into tools the school has checked, with access controls and the minimum necessary. They never go into consumer chat apps. FERPA and state student-privacy rules differ, so check yours. These pages are general information. They are not educational, legal, or compliance advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with FERPA or any other rule. U.S. Department of Education FERPA guidance is a starting point.
