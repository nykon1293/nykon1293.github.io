Use cases for medical and healthcare practices

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) can help with this kind of work. Scheduling, reminders, intake, and billing admin for small practices. Clinicians and staff review and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the business side of a healthcare practice. That means scheduling, reminders, intake logistics, insurance and billing admin, referral tracking, and communications drafts. SI does not name a condition. SI does not give medical advice. SI does not make clinical decisions. SI does not replace a clinician's judgment. A clinician or trained staff member reviews and approves anything clinical or patient-facing before it goes out. Nothing here promises any health outcome or result. Patient privacy comes first. Protected health information (PHI) should only go into tools covered by a signed business associate agreement (BAA) with the practice. Use proper access controls. Use only the minimum necessary. PHI never belongs in consumer tools or free chat apps. Billing and coding are always approved by a person. SI never invents documentation. SI never assigns codes on its own. Recording or transcribing calls and visits may require consent. Texting patients is regulated. State laws differ, so check yours. SI never handles emergencies. Anything urgent goes to a person or to 911. These pages are general information. They are not medical, legal, or compliance advice. They are not a guarantee of compliance with HIPAA or any other rule. HHS guidance on business associates is a good starting point.

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you.
