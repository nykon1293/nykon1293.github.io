Use cases for medical and healthcare practices

Scheduling, reminders, intake, and billing admin for small practices. Clinicians and staff review and decide.

Read this first. The use cases in this category are about running the business side of a healthcare practice: scheduling, reminders, intake logistics, insurance and billing admin, referral tracking, and communications drafts. SI does not name a condition, give medical advice, or make clinical decisions, and it does not replace a clinician's judgment. A clinician or trained staff member reviews and approves anything clinical or patient-facing before it goes out, and nothing here promises any health outcome or result. Patient privacy comes first: protected health information (PHI) should only go into tools covered by a signed business associate agreement (BAA) with the practice, with proper access controls, and only the minimum necessary; it never belongs in consumer tools or free chat apps. Billing and coding are always approved by a person; SI never invents documentation or assigns codes on its own. Recording or transcribing calls and visits may require consent, texting patients is regulated, and state laws differ, so check yours. SI never handles emergencies: anything urgent goes to a person or to 911. These pages are general information, not medical, legal or compliance advice, and not a guarantee of compliance with HIPAA or any other rule. HHS guidance on business associates is a good starting point.
