Session notes into progress updates for families or clients

After each session or class, SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) uses the instructor's quick notes. It writes a short, consistent progress update. A parent or client can then receive it.

Worth knowing: If sessions are therapy, counseling, or medical, the notes are protected health information. Use tools that meet your privacy rules. A clinician must review them.

The problem

Instructors and coaches jot notes. They rarely have time to write updates. Families or clients hear little until there is a problem. The updates that do go out vary a lot.

What SI does

SI takes the instructor's notes or voice memo. It writes a short update in a consistent format. The update names what was taught and what went well. It also names what to practice and what comes next. It keeps a running record per learner so trends are visible. It flags notes that suggest a concern. The instructor handles those personally.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Tutoring program

    A math tutor's two-line note becomes a parent update. It covers fractions practice and next week's focus.

  • Music teacher

    Lesson notes become a weekly message. It lists the pieces to practice and a tip for the tricky passage.

  • Driving instructor

    Each lesson produces a short summary of skills covered. It also lists what to practice before the road test.

What it takes to set up

A simple notes form or voice memo path, a template, and contacts. Setup is often a day or two.

What a human still checks

  • The instructor approves each update before it is sent.
  • Concerns are communicated personally, not by template.
  • Notes about minors are stored with limited access.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Turn these session notes [paste] for [learner] into a short update for [parent/client] with what was covered, what went well, what to practice and what is next, using this template [example]. Flag any note that suggests a concern. Add it to the running record [file]. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Families and clients stay informed. Instructors spend less time writing.
