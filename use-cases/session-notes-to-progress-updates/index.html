The problem

After class or a program hour, the update to a family is easy to skip. Notes stay in a notebook. Families hear nothing until a problem shows up.

What SI does

SI uses the notes the teacher or staff member just wrote. It drafts a short update in a steady format. A nonprofit program can use the same draft for a family note. SI does not grade or place a student. A person approves anything sent to a student or parent.

Related in this library: Tutoring session notes and parent progress summaries, Parent email drafts in the teacher's own voice, Report-card comment drafts from the teacher's notes.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Teacher

    SI drafts a progress line from the teacher's own notes. The teacher approves it before it goes to a parent.

  • After-school program

    SI drafts a family update from the coach's notes. A staff member sends it.

  • Community learning nonprofit

    Weekly notes become a short family update. A coordinator reviews it first.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. The school reviews each tool before it holds student records. Check the vendor's terms, access controls, and any FERPA or state student-privacy duties. Start with sample or de-identified material. Use a simple notes form or voice-memo path. Use the teacher's or program's summary template. Permission is to draft, not to send. Setup is often a day or two.

What a human still checks

  • A person approves anything sent to a student or parent.
  • The teacher or staff member reviews each draft.
  • Concerns are handled by a person, not by a template.
  • SI never sends, posts, or changes a record on its own.
  • Notes about minors stay on the school's or program's own accounts.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Turn these session notes [paste] into a short family update using this template [paste]: what was practiced, what to review, and what is next. Use only facts the teacher or staff member wrote. Flag notes that sound like a concern for a person to handle. Pause and show me before sending, posting or changing anything. Use only the sample or de-identified information I provide; do not include real student names, grades, or family contact details.

Typical effect

Families get a steady update, and a person still sends it.
