Real estate

Rental application completeness checklist

Each application is checked against the manager's own form for missing items, with a neutral request draft and a log in the order received.

Admin and operations help for agents, landlords and small property managers. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner; they review and decide. SI does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Worth knowing: Administrative support only: SI does not give legal or lease advice, make leasing, screening or pricing decisions, or act as an agent or broker. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants. A licensed professional or the property owner reviews and approves anything client-facing, tenant-facing or contractual. Fair housing laws apply: SI never screens, ranks, steers or targets by protected characteristics, and applicant and tenant information is used only in checked tools with access controls and the minimum necessary. SI never reviews, scores, ranks, recommends or denies an applicant, never evaluates credit, background or income, and never filters on any protected characteristic or its proxies; the manager applies written criteria equally to everyone and gets legal advice on screening rules.

The problem

Applications come in with missing pages, and managers spend time chasing documents.

What SI does

SI generates a checklist from the manager's own application form (fields completed, documents requested, fee receipt noted), drafts a neutral we-are-still-missing-[item] request using the manager's wording, and logs the status of each application in the order received. SI prepares drafts for the licensed agent or owner and does not lease, collect rent, screen or rank applicants.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Small property-management company

    An application for [Unit] is checked against the manager's form. A missing-item draft uses the manager's wording and waits for approval.

  • Landlord with a few rental units

    Applications are logged in the order received. SI does not score or rank anyone.

  • Independent real estate agent

    A completeness checklist lists fields and documents. The owner reviews the file and makes every decision.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds applicant or tenant information, the business checks the tool's terms and data practices, sets access controls and limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use the manager's application form and approved missing-item wording. Start with sample applications. Permission is to checklist and draft, not to decide. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The agent, broker or manager approves before anything goes to a client, tenant, applicant or vendor.
  • SI never sends, posts, signs or changes records on its own.
  • Unusual items go to a "needs manager or attorney review" queue.
  • The business decides what personal data SI may see, and testing starts with sample information.
  • The manager sets written, consistent screening criteria, applies them to every applicant the same way, reviews each application, and makes and communicates every decision, including any required notice if an application is denied.

Typical effect

Missing items are listed in the order applications arrived, and every decision stays with the manager.
