Reporting & dashboards

Ad spend next to real results, in one sheet

SI lines up spend from each ad platform with the inquiries, bookings or sales it produced, and lists campaigns that look weak.

Worth knowing: Law firms and health practices: advertising is subject to professional and privacy rules, and tracking tools must not send protected client or patient information to ad platforms.

The problem

Each ad platform reports its own success, often generously. Comparing spend with actual customers means stitching exports together by hand.

What SI does

SI pulls spend by campaign from each platform and matches it to results in your own systems (form leads, booked calls, sales by source or promo code). It shows cost per lead or sale by campaign and week, and lists campaigns with spend but little real result, with suggestions to review.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Personal injury law firm

    Search ad spend by campaign is matched to signed intake forms rather than clicks, showing which campaigns bring qualified calls.

  • Home cleaning service

    Social ads in two towns produce plenty of leads but few booked cleanings; the sheet lists them for review.

  • Online store

    Spend by campaign is compared with orders using the store's source tracking, alongside the platforms' own numbers.

What it takes to set up

Read access or exports from the ad platforms and your lead or sales system, plus consistent tracking such as source fields or codes. Setup is often about a week.

What a human still checks

  • Budget changes are made by a person; SI only recommends.
  • Attribution gaps are stated plainly.
  • Recommendations are reviewed with whoever runs the ads.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Combine ad spend by campaign and week from [platforms/exports] with results from [lead or sales system] matched by [source, campaign tag or code]. Show cost per [lead/booking/sale] by campaign. List campaigns with spend above [amount] and few results, with suggestions. Note any gaps in tracking. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Ad decisions rest on real customers, and weak campaigns are easier to spot.
