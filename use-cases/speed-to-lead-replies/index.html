Speed-to-lead replies for new inquiries

When a new inquiry arrives, SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) drafts a specific first reply right away. It leaves the draft for a person to send.

The problem

New inquiries sit for hours while the day gets busy. The person calls the next business. Turning an inquiry into a CRM record keeps the lead. It does not answer them.

What SI does

SI watches the form, inbox, or voicemail notes you point it at. For a new inquiry it pulls out what they asked. It also pulls the contact and any date or address. Then it drafts a short first reply in your voice. The draft says you got it, the next step, and one question you still need. It does not invent open times, prices, or promises. After-hours inquiries are queued for the morning with the draft ready. A person sends it.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Contractor

    A form asks for a fence estimate with the city and a photo. SI drafts a reply that confirms the city is in the service area. It asks for the missing gate width. The owner sends it.

  • Tutor

    An evening email asks about algebra help. A draft is waiting in the morning. It uses the subjects you listed and asks for the student's grade. It does not claim that a specific hour is open.

  • Clinic

    A new-patient form arrives at lunch. SI drafts a reply with what to bring to a first visit. The list comes from your intake notes. It leaves insurance questions for the front desk.

What it takes to set up

The place inquiries arrive. A few replies you liked. The facts the draft may use. That includes hours, service area, and what you need from them. A rule for what must go to a person with no draft. Emergencies and complaints are examples. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • A person sends every first reply.
  • SI does not quote a price or a time. It uses only the material you gave it.
  • Emergencies and complaints are flagged for a person instead of getting a routine draft.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Watch new inquiries in [inbox or form]. For each one, pull out the ask, contact, and any date, address, or other detail. Draft a short first reply using only these facts and this tone [paste examples and allowed facts]. Do not invent prices, open times, or promises. Flag emergencies and complaints for me with no routine draft. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

New inquiries get a specific draft while the request is still fresh. A person still sends it.
