Scheduling

Turning meeting requests and forwarded emails into calendar holds

When an email asks to meet or includes a date, SI drafts the reply with real open times and creates a tentative calendar hold for you to confirm.

Worth knowing: Legal deadlines: SI-created entries are a convenience, not a docketing system. Court and filing deadlines must be confirmed by the attorney.

The problem

Scheduling takes several back-and-forth emails, and dates buried in emails, flyers and attachments never make it onto the calendar.

What SI does

SI reads incoming messages and forwarded items for meeting requests, deadlines and event dates. For meeting requests it checks your calendar and drafts a reply with a few times that fit your rules. For dates in forwarded emails or attachments, it creates tentative events with the details and a link back to the source.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Real estate agent

    A buyer's email asking to see two houses on Saturday becomes a draft reply with three time windows and tentative holds.

  • Lawyer in solo practice

    A forwarded court notice becomes a calendar entry with the hearing date and a reminder a week before.

  • School parent association

    Dates from a forwarded school newsletter become tentative events on the association's calendar.

What it takes to set up

Calendar and email access with draft permissions, plus your scheduling preferences. Setup is often a day.

What a human still checks

  • Holds stay tentative until you confirm.
  • Replies are approved before sending.
  • SI does not accept invitations on your behalf.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Read [inbox/forwarded messages]. For each meeting request, draft a reply offering [3] times that fit [rules] in my calendar. For each date or deadline mentioned, create a tentative event with the details and a link to the source. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Less back-and-forth to set meetings, and fewer dates fall off the calendar.
