Shipping exceptions and a 'where is my package' watch

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) watches carrier scans for exceptions, failed deliveries, and stalled tracking. It prepares a short list plus a draft note before the customer has to ask.

Worth knowing: Carrier tracking can lag. A missing scan is not always a lost package.

The problem

The first time many stores learn a package failed delivery or sat at a depot is when the customer writes. They ask where the package is. Drafting a status reply from your own records helps once they write in. It does not watch carrier exceptions before that. A daily low-stock and stuck-order list is a different job.

What SI does

SI reads tracking for open shipments from the store, a shipping tool, or an export. It lists exception scans, failed delivery, address issues, and tracking with no scan past a wait you set. For each, it notes the last scan, the order, and a suggested next step. Next steps can include asking the customer to confirm the address, contacting the carrier, or waiting. It can draft a customer note from the real tracking. It does not change addresses or file claims.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Shopify store using a shipping app

    Two packages show a delivery exception and one has no scan in five days. The list and draft notes wait for the owner.

  • Beauty brand shipping two-day

    A failed delivery marked 'need new address' is listed with the order. A draft asks the customer to confirm the door code.

  • Outdoor gear shop

    Weekend scans lag, so SI still lists exceptions. It flags ones that already have a customer email so you do not double-reply.

What it takes to set up

You need tracking numbers for open shipments. You also need carrier or shipping-tool access or a daily export, and a wait threshold. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • A person decides whether to contact the customer, the carrier, or wait.
  • SI does not promise a delivery date that is not on the scan.
  • Address changes and claims are made by a person.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
From open shipments in [store or shipping export], list tracking with an exception scan, failed delivery, address issue, or no scan in [5] days. For each, show order number, last scan, and a suggested next step. Draft a customer note using only the tracking on file. Do not change addresses or file claims. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

Exceptions show up as a list you can work. That is better than a pile of 'where is my package' emails.
