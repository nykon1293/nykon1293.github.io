The problem

A lot sold for one cash total. The folder still shows those cards as on hand. Typing each sale by hand is easy to skip. Later a new slab looks like a duplicate of a card that already sold.

What SI does

You name the cards that sold and the total cash for the lot. SI drafts sold marks in your PriceCharting folder. It splits the total across those cards by guide value so the sales history stays in line with the guide. Unsold cards stay in the folder. Later, SI checks new slabs against sold ones so nothing is counted twice. You approve before anything is saved.

Related in this library: Keep a live collection on PriceCharting, Know collection value before a show, Price a card before you buy.

Examples

The same job looks different for different collectors. These are examples, not client results.

  • TCG collector after a show

    Three Pokémon cards sold as a lot. SI drafts sold marks on PriceCharting and splits the cash total by guide value. The rest of the binder is unchanged.

  • Collector who trades and sells

    A Magic card that sold last month is in a new photo. SI flags it as already sold so it is not added twice.

  • Collector with a sports folder

    Sold sports cards are drafted on SportsCardsPro. TCG stays on PriceCharting.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Your PriceCharting login stays yours. SportsCardsPro uses the same account. GemmiSI does not hold the collection. Show SI how you mark a sale today. Permission is to draft sold marks, not to save them. We sit with you, plan the steps, and teach you to run it. Setup is often a few days.
