Customer-service macros drafted from past tickets

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) groups past helpdesk tickets. It turns the clearest replies into draft macros with the merge fields you use. Publishing the macros is left to you.

The problem

Agents rewrite the same shipping, sizing, and order-status answers every day. Building an FAQ from past replies helps customers who read a help page. It does not put a checked reply one click away in the helpdesk.

What SI does

SI reads an export of tickets or macros you already have. It groups similar questions, such as shipping times, how to start a return, sizing, and missing item. It picks the clearest past reply. It drafts a macro in your voice with placeholders for order number, tracking, and name. It notes where past agents disagreed so you can pick a policy. It does not publish macros or send them.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Shopify store using a helpdesk

    'Where is my order' and 'how do I start a return' become two macros with order and tracking fields. They come from the replies staff already send.

  • Beauty brand

    Shade-match threads produce three drafts. One is flagged because past answers promised a result the policy does not.

  • Outdoor shop

    A 'will it arrive by Friday' macro is drafted to use only the carrier date already on the order.

What it takes to set up

You need a few months of ticket export or existing macros. Add your policy pages and the merge-field names your helpdesk uses. Setup is often a few days plus editing.

What a human still checks

  • A person edits and publishes every macro.
  • Conflicting past answers are resolved by a person, not averaged.
  • Refund, discount, and legal wording is checked before a macro goes live.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Read these tickets or macros [export]. Group questions that come up more than [3] times. For each group, draft a helpdesk macro from the clearest past reply, in this voice [examples], with merge fields [order number, tracking, name]. List groups where past answers disagree. Do not publish or send. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

Agents start from a checked reply instead of rewriting the same paragraph. The public FAQ can stay a separate page.
