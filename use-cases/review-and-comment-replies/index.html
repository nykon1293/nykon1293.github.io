The problem

Reviews and comments arrive on several platforms. Answering them slips. Negative reviews sit unanswered for weeks. Genuine questions in comments get lost among the noise.

What SI does

SI collects new reviews and comments. It sorts them into praise, question, complaint, and spam. It drafts short replies for the ones that need one. Complaints get a careful, non-defensive draft. That draft offers to take the talk offline. Complaints are flagged for the owner. SI never argues. SI never reveals private details. A contractor, a clinic, and a shop can use the same drafts. You still post them.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Family restaurant

    A two-star review about a long wait gets a calm draft. It acknowledges the night, without excuses. It invites the guest to contact the manager.

  • Auto body shop

    Questions in social comments about insurance claims are flagged. The draft points people to call for an estimate.

  • Dental practice

    Positive reviews get short thank-you drafts. Those drafts avoid confirming the reviewer is a patient.

  • Two-dentist practice

    A review-request draft goes to the whole recent-visit list, not a hand-picked happy subset. Staff approve it before it is sent.

  • Med spa

    A public reply draft thanks the reviewer and gives the practice phone number. It does not say the reviewer was a patient. It includes no before-and-after language.

  • Independent chiropractic clinic

    Reply options for a public comment invite a phone call and mention no care. A person posts only the version they approve.

What it takes to set up

Access or exports from the review and social platforms you use. Plus a few examples of replies you like. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • Every public reply is approved by a person before posting.
  • Complaints go straight to the owner. So does anything mentioning safety, injury, or legal threats.
  • Replies never include private customer details.
  • A staff member or clinician approves before anything goes to a patient, a payer, or any outside party.
  • SI never sends, submits, bills, or posts on its own.
  • Anything unusual goes to a "needs clinician or manager review" queue.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Here are new reviews and comments from [platforms] since [date]: [paste or export]. Sort them into praise, question, complaint and spam. Draft a reply for each praise, question and complaint in this tone: [examples]. Keep complaints calm, short and offer to continue privately. Do not mention any personal details. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Reviews and comments get answered more often in the same way. The owner sees complaints early.
