Trainer onboarding notes and reminders

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) drafts a training-schedule PDF and reminder notes for a new client. The trainer approves them before they are sent.

The problem

A new client starts, and the plan stays in the trainer's head. The PDF goes out late. Reminder notes are missed.

What SI does

SI reads the plan the trainer already wrote and the start date. It drafts a simple schedule as a PDF and a few reminder notes in the trainer's wording. The trainer approves the PDF and each note before they are sent. SI does not send them, change the plan, or give health advice.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Independent trainer

    A new client starts Monday. SI drafts a four-week PDF from the trainer's plan. The trainer sends it after a read.

  • Small studio

    SI drafts a reminder for the first three sessions. The coach edits one line and sends the notes.

  • Youth sports coach

    The plan has a blank week. SI leaves that week blank and flags it. The coach fills it before the PDF goes out.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Use the plan template and reminder wording the trainer already likes. Permission is to draft, not to send. We sit with you, plan the steps, and teach you to run it. Setup is often a day or two while you check one sample client.

What a human still checks

  • The trainer approves the PDF and every note before they are sent.
  • SI does not change the training plan or give health advice.
  • A person sends anything that goes to the client.
  • Texting rules vary. Get consent and honor opt-outs.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
From this training plan [paste] and start date [date], draft a schedule PDF and reminder notes in this wording [paste]. Do not change the plan, give health advice, or send. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.
