A website or messaging chat assistant that hands hard cases to a person

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) answers common questions in chat from your own website and documents. It passes anything unusual to a person. The chat is attached.

Worth knowing: Law, medical, and financial offices: the assistant should share general office facts only. It should never share anything that reads as advice on a specific situation.

The problem

People ask the same questions in chat or messaging apps at all hours. Either someone answers late. Or the chat widget is a form that just says we will get back to you.

What SI does

SI answers from an approved set of sources. Those sources are your website, FAQ, price list, and policies. It can collect details for a quote or booking request. When a question falls outside its sources, it stops. It also stops if the question involves a complaint. It also stops if the person asks for a human. It says someone will follow up. Then it sends the chat summary to staff.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Landscaping company

    A visitor asks whether spring cleanups are still available in their town. SI confirms the service area. It collects the address and yard size. Then it sends the request to the office.

  • Immigration law office

    SI answers questions about office hours, consultation fees, and what documents to bring. It hands any question about a specific case to staff.

  • Adult language classes

    SI explains class levels and start dates. It collects placement-test requests.

What it takes to set up

An approved knowledge source. The chat or messaging tool. Clear rules for handoff. Setup is often one to two weeks. That includes testing with real questions.

What a human still checks

  • SI answers only from approved sources. It says so when it does not know.
  • Anything involving money, complaints, or exceptions is handed to a person.
  • Staff review a sample of chats each week. They correct answers.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using only these sources [website pages, FAQ, price list], answer customer questions in [chat/WhatsApp]. If a question is not covered, involves a complaint or money, or the person asks for a human, say that someone will follow up within [time] and send a summary to [staff contact]. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Common questions get answered when the office is closed. Staff pick up the chats that need them. They have the context in hand.
