Donor and scholarship thank-you drafts in the school's own wording

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) drafts thank-you notes for gifts and scholarships from the school's saved wording and holds them until after Shabbat or Yom Tov.

Admin help for teachers, tutors and schools. SI drafts and organizes; a person approves anything sent to a student or parent. No grading decisions, and student records stay on the school's own equipment and accounts.

Worth knowing: Administrative support only: SI does not grade, evaluate, or make academic decisions, and does not replace a teacher's or school's judgment. A teacher, tutor, or school staff member reviews and approves anything sent to a student or parent. Student records are used only on the school's own equipment and accounts, with access controls and the minimum necessary, never in consumer tools. Donor and scholarship records are confidential. SI fills the school's wording; it does not solicit, value a gift, or claim a tax result. Nothing sends, posts, or runs on Shabbat or Yom Tov; drafts wait in a queue until after. No images of people. FERPA still applies if a student scholarship is named.

The problem

Thank-you notes for gifts and scholarship funds wait until someone has time. Wording has to stay modest and in the school's voice, and a note must not go out on Shabbat or Yom Tov.

What SI does

SI reads the gift or scholarship-acknowledgment list the office provides and fills the school's approved thank-you wording with the designation and amount the office already recorded. Notes wait in a queue until after Shabbat or Yom Tov. SI does not invent results, does not attach images of people, keeps a modest tone, and does not write religious rulings or Judaic lesson content.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school

    A scholarship-fund gift the office already recorded is filled into the approved thank-you wording and held until after Shabbat.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school development office

    A community-event pledge list becomes draft notes from the school's sentences only. No photograph is added.

  • Orthodox Jewish day school business office

    Year-end acknowledgment drafts use the amount and designation already in the file. Staff send them after Yom Tov.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds student records, the school reviews the vendor's terms, access controls, and any FERPA or state student-privacy duties. Start with sample or de-identified material. The school's approved thank-you wording and a de-identified gift list. Permission is to draft, not to send. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The development or business office reviews every note before a donor or a parent receives it.
  • Nothing sends, posts, or runs on Shabbat or Yom Tov; approved messages wait in a queue until after.
  • SI does not create or attach images of people.
  • SI does not write religious rulings or Judaic lesson content, and it does not invent how a gift was used.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Using this gift list [file] and this approved thank-you wording [paste], draft a note for each row with only the designation and amount already recorded. Do not invent how the gift was used. Do not add photographs. Pause and show me before sending, posting or changing anything. Use only the sample or de-identified information I provide; do not include real student names, grades, or family contact details. Do not send, post, or run anything on Shabbat or Yom Tov; queue drafts for after. Do not create or attach images of people. Do not write religious rulings or Judaic lesson content.
