The problem

The map listing shows last season's hours, a closed holiday as open, or services you no longer offer. Questions on the listing sit unanswered. Refreshing your website does not update the listing customers see in maps and search.

What SI does

SI compares the listing details you export or paste with the source you treat as current. Details include hours, holiday hours, services, description, phone, and website. It lists what is stale and drafts the corrected text. It drafts answers to new questions from your FAQ or policy. It drafts a short post from a real update you provide, such as a holiday closure or a new service. It does not publish anything. It can draft holiday hours for each listing you name. You still post them.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Restaurant

    Holiday hours on the listing still match a normal week. SI drafts the corrected hours and a short closure post for the owner to publish.

  • Dental office

    A question about Saturday hours is drafted from the posted schedule. A service you stopped offering is flagged because it is still on the listing.

  • Home-services company

    The service list on the profile is compared with the current menu of jobs. Missing services are drafted as edits for the owner to apply.

  • Neighborhood restaurant

    Thanksgiving hours are closed on Google but still a normal dinner service on Facebook and the website. SI drafts the matching closure text for each place.

  • Farm stand

    Peak-season Saturday hours were extended in the reservation sheet. They were never copied to Apple Maps or the hours page.

  • Hair salon

    December 24 is a short day in the appointment book. SI lists Google, Instagram, and the booking site as still showing a full day. It drafts hours for each.

What it takes to set up

You need a copy of the current listing details. Add the hours and services you want shown, and a few posts or answers you liked. Access stays with the person who manages the profile. Setup is often a day, then a repeating check.
