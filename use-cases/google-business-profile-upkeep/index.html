Google Business Profile upkeep

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) checks your Google Business Profile against the hours, services, and details you actually use, and drafts updates and replies for you to post.

The problem

The map listing shows last season's hours, a closed holiday as open, or services you no longer offer. Questions on the listing sit unanswered. Refreshing your website does not update the listing customers see in maps and search.

What SI does

SI compares the listing details you export or paste (hours, holiday hours, services, description, phone, website) with the source you treat as current. It lists what is stale and drafts the corrected text. It drafts answers to new questions from your FAQ or policy, and a short post from a real update you provide (a holiday closure, a new service). It does not publish anything.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Restaurant

    Holiday hours on the listing still match a normal week. SI drafts the corrected hours and a short closure post for the owner to publish.

  • Dental office

    A question about Saturday hours is drafted from the posted schedule, and a service you stopped offering is flagged because it is still on the listing.

  • Home-services company

    The service list on the profile is compared with the current menu of jobs, and missing services are drafted as edits for the owner to apply.

What it takes to set up

A copy of the current listing details, the hours and services you want shown, and a few posts or answers you liked. Access stays with the person who manages the profile. Setup is often a day, then a repeating check.

What a human still checks

  • A person posts every change, answer, and update on the listing.
  • Hours and holiday closures are confirmed against the real calendar.
  • SI does not invent reviews, ratings, or claims about rankings.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool of choice and swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Compare this Google Business Profile copy [paste] with the current hours, services, phone, and website [paste]. List what is stale and draft the corrected text. Draft answers to these questions [paste] using only [FAQ or policy]. Draft one post from this update [paste]. Do not invent reviews, ratings, or ranking claims. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting, or changing anything.

Typical effect

Hours, services, questions, and posts on the listing are drafted from your current facts, and a person publishes them.
