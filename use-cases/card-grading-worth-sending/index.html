The problem

Grading costs money and time. Raw and graded sales tell different stories. The collector does not want to send a card that will not cover the fee.

What SI does

SI reads the fees and turnaround you point it at. It compares those with raw vs graded sales for cards in your folder. It drafts a short list of cards that look worth sending. It can note a local drop-off option you already use. It checks fees weekly for changes. After cards come back, it drafts folder updates with grade and cert. You pick the company. You decide what to send.

Related in this library: Keep a live collection on PriceCharting, Know collection value before a show, Price a card before you buy.

Examples

The same job looks different for different collectors. These are examples, not client results.

  • Collector sorting a raw stack

    SI lists a few folder cards whose graded sales look stronger than raw after the fee. The collector still picks which ones go.

  • Collector with a local drop-off

    The note includes the drop-off the collector already named. SI does not book it.

  • Collector getting slabs back

    Grades and certs the collector provides are drafted onto the PriceCharting entries. Nothing saves until they approve.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Your PriceCharting login stays yours. SportsCardsPro uses the same account. GemmiSI does not hold the collection. Point SI at the grading companies you actually use. Share fee pages and a local drop-off if you have one. Permission is to list and draft, not to submit an order. We sit with you, plan the steps, and teach you to run it. Setup is often a few days.
