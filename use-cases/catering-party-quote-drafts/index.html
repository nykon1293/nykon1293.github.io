Catering and party requests turned into quote drafts

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) turns catering and party requests into quote drafts with date, headcount, menu, and deposit. A person still sets the price before anything goes out.

Worth knowing: Quote drafts wait for the owner. SI does not set the price.

The problem

Party requests sit in email and texts while date, headcount, and menu notes are spread across messages. A quote then goes out late, or it goes out with a missing fact the owner has to fix.

What SI does

SI reads the request and pulls the date, headcount, menu notes, and any deposit rule you already use. It drafts a quote from your rate sheet and flags facts that are still missing so the owner can fill them in. It does not send the quote, take a deposit, or invent a new price.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Catering kitchen

    A request for 40 people on a Saturday becomes a draft with the platter list and the usual deposit, and the owner checks the price.

  • Restaurant with a private room

    An email for a birthday dinner pulls the date and headcount, and the owner adds the room fee before it is sent.

  • Bakery that does parties

    A cake and drop-off request becomes a draft from the cake list, and a missing pickup time is flagged for the owner.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Use the inbox or form where requests arrive, and add your menu, rate sheet, and deposit rule. Permission is to draft, not to send or take money. Setup is often a few days while you check a few real requests against the draft.

What a human still checks

  • The owner checks the date, headcount, and price before anything goes out.
  • SI does not set a new price or take a deposit on its own.
  • Missing dates or counts wait for a person instead of being guessed.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Read this catering or party request [paste]. Pull date, headcount, menu notes, and deposit using these rates [file]. Draft a quote. Flag missing facts. Do not send it or change prices. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

A quote draft waits with the date, headcount, and menu already pulled out, and the owner still prices it.
