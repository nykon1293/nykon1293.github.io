Refreshing tired web pages and search snippets

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) reviews your key web pages. It looks for old details, weak titles, and missing information. It proposes specific rewrites you approve.

The problem

Service and category pages were written years ago. Hours, prices, and services are out of date. Titles and descriptions in search results are vague. Important questions are not answered.

What SI does

SI reads your main pages. It checks for outdated details. It checks for missing basics such as service area, hours, and how to book. It checks unclear headings and weak page titles and meta descriptions. It proposes rewrites for each page in a change sheet. It drafts a few short articles that answer common customer questions.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Chimney sweep

    Service pages get clear titles. Titles name the services and towns covered. A new page answers how often chimneys should be inspected.

  • Family law practice

    Practice-area pages are rewritten in plainer language. They include a clear consultation process. An attorney reviews them.

  • Garden center

    Seasonal category pages are refreshed. They include current plant availability and planting tips.

What it takes to set up

A list of pages. You need the site editor or a person to apply changes. You also need the current facts. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The owner approves every change.
  • SI does not change live pages without approval.
  • The owner confirms facts like prices and services.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Review these pages [URLs]. For each, list outdated details, missing basics [service area, hours, booking], and weak headings. Propose a new page title, meta description and rewritten sections in a change sheet. Suggest [3] short articles answering common questions [list]. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

The website is current and clearer. That helps both visitors and search results.
