After-hours call and message sorting

Overnight messages are sorted into queues, with gas, fire, smoke or flooding flagged for the owner and one approved acknowledgment.

Office help for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, pool and cleaning businesses. SI drafts, tracks and reminds; the owner approves every quote and message. No pricing promises, no safety or code advice, and customers' information stays private.

Worth knowing: Office support only: SI does not set or promise prices, give safety, repair, code or insurance advice, or make any commitment for the business. The owner or an authorized person reviews and approves every quote, schedule commitment and customer message. Licensing, advertising, deposit and permit rules vary by state, and customer information is used only in checked tools with access controls. SI never gives safety instructions, troubleshoots hazards or tells a caller what to do, never says the business will arrive at a time, and is not an emergency service; the approved acknowledgment is the only auto-reply. Texting rules vary; get consent and honor opt-outs.

The problem

Calls and web forms arrive at night, and a true emergency can sit next to a routine request.

What SI does

SI sorts overnight messages into queues (new job request, existing customer, billing, supplier, spam), pulls out name, address, contact and the problem as described, flags messages mentioning gas, fire, smoke, flooding or electrical hazards to the top for the owner, and sends one approved acknowledgment: the business will follow up, and for an emergency call 911 or the utility.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are illustrations, not client results.

  • Small HVAC company

    Overnight messages are split into new job, existing customer, billing, supplier, and spam. Mentions of gas or no heat with smoke are flagged to the top.

  • Solo plumber

    The approved acknowledgment says the business will follow up and to call 911 or the utility in an emergency.

  • Two-crew roofing business

    Name, address, and the problem as described are pulled out. SI does not say the crew will arrive at a time.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds customer information, the business checks the tool's terms and data practices, sets access controls and limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Connect the inbox or web form with permission to read and draft, not to dispatch. Save the acknowledgment the owner has already approved. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The owner or office manager approves before anything goes to a customer, vendor or inspector.
  • SI never sends a quote, takes a payment, books a job or submits anything on its own.
  • Urgent or unusual items go to a "needs owner review" queue.
  • The business decides what customer data SI may see, and testing starts with sample information.
  • The owner or on-call person reads the queue first thing, decides who gets a callback and when, and handles every emergency dispatch.
