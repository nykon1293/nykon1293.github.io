Gift-card and holiday promo tracking

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) lines up outstanding gift cards and holiday promo codes from your POS export. You can see what is still open. It does not change balances.

Book a free 30-minute consult

Worth knowing: Gift-card expiry, breakage, and unclaimed-property rules vary. SI lists what is in your export. It does not give legal or tax advice.

The problem

Gift cards sold in December and promo codes from a holiday weekend live in the POS, a spreadsheet, and someone's memory. Subscriptions and renewals track recurring plans. Sales-tax prep notes are for the accountant. Neither is a list of outstanding gift cards and holiday codes the shop still has to honor.

What SI does

SI reads the gift-card and discount-code export you provide. It lists outstanding gift-card balances, codes still marked active, codes past the end date you recorded, and totals by type (gift card vs promo). It flags rows with no end date or a balance that does not match the last activity line. It does not change balances, expire codes, charge anyone, or file anything.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Neighborhood restaurant

    December gift cards still open are listed with the remaining balance as printed in the POS. Codes with no end date are set aside.

  • Spa that sells holiday packages

    Holiday promo codes still marked active after the weekend you named are listed next to the end date in the file. The owner can turn them off.

  • Toy and gift shop

    Outstanding gift cards from last December are totaled by month of sale as they appear in the export. Unused cards that have no activity are flagged for review.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. You need a gift-card and discount-code export from the POS or booking tool. Add a note on which statuses count as open. Setup is often a day or two after the holiday rush, then a monthly refresh.

What a human still checks

  • A person checks that balances and dates match the POS before relying on the list.
  • A person approves anything that sends, pays, orders, or posts.
  • SI does not expire codes, change balances, or take payment.

Typical effect

Outstanding gift cards and holiday codes are a list from your own export. A person still decides what to honor or turn off.
