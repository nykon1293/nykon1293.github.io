Permit and inspection tracking

The owner enters each job's permit and inspection dates. Those dates sit in a tracker. SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) adds reminders and routine status-email drafts.

Office help for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, pool, and cleaning businesses. SI drafts, tracks, and reminds. The owner approves every quote and message. No price promises. No safety or code advice. Customer information stays private.

Worth knowing: This is office support only. SI does not set prices. SI does not promise prices. SI does not give safety, repair, code, or insurance advice. SI does not make any commitment for the business. The owner or an authorized person reviews every quote, schedule commitment, and customer message. They must approve it. License, ad, deposit, and permit rules vary by state. Customer information is used only in checked tools with access controls. SI tracks only what the owner enters. SI never says whether a permit is required. SI never says whether work passes code. SI never says what a deadline legally is. Permit, deposit, and start-date rules vary by state and city. Check yours.

The problem

Permits and inspections are missed or forgotten. That stalls jobs and can cause problems later.

What SI does

SI keeps a tracker per job. Fields include permit needed per the owner, applied date, permit number, inspection dates, and status. SI reminds the office of next steps and stale items. SI drafts routine status emails to the permitting office for the owner to send.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Two-crew roofing business

    A tracker for [Job] lists the permit number and inspection dates the owner entered. Stale items are flagged for the office.

  • Small HVAC company

    A routine status-email draft to the permitting office waits for the owner to send.

  • Solo plumber

    Next-step reminders cover only dates the owner supplied. SI does not say whether a permit is required.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Before connecting anything that holds customer information, the business checks the tool's terms. It also checks data practices. It sets access controls. It limits SI to the minimum necessary. Start with sample or made-up information. Use only dates and permit numbers the owner has entered. SI does not decide whether a permit is required. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • The owner or office manager approves before anything goes to a customer, vendor, or inspector.
  • SI never sends a quote, takes a payment, books a job, or submits anything on its own.
  • Urgent or unusual items go to a "needs owner review" queue.
  • The business decides what customer data SI may see. Testing starts with sample information.
  • The owner or licensed qualifier decides which permits are required. That person pulls and signs the permits. That person attends or schedules inspections.
