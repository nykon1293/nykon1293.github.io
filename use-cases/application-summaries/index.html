The problem

A posting brings in dozens or hundreds of applications. Busy managers skim. They miss good candidates. They apply different standards on different days.

What SI does

SI reads each application. It writes a digest against the written, job-related criteria. Criteria include required license or certification. They also include relevant experience, availability, and answers to screening questions. SI notes where information is missing. It groups applications into meets criteria, partly meets, and does not show the requirement. Each group includes the reason. SI does not reject anyone. The manager reviews.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Home care agency

    SI writes a digest of caregiver applications. It covers certification, driving, and weekend availability. Missing certification dates are noted.

  • Electrical contractor

    SI writes a digest of journeyman applicants. It covers license status and type of work. The owner calls the ones who meet the requirements.

  • Private school

    SI writes a digest of teaching applications. It covers subject certification and grade-level experience. The principal reviews them.

What it takes to set up

The role's written criteria. Access to applications from the job board or applicant tracker. Setup is often a day or two per role type.

What a human still checks

  • A person reviews every application group and makes all decisions.
  • SI ignores names, photos, ages, and other protected characteristics in its summaries.
  • Criteria and outcomes are reviewed from time to time so they stay the same and fair.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
Here are the written criteria for [role]: [paste]. Summarize each application in [folder/applicant tracker] against those criteria only, ignoring names, photos, age and other personal characteristics. Group them into meets, partly meets and does not show the requirement, with a one-line reason and any missing information. Do not reject anyone. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Managers spend their time on the most relevant applicants. The first read is the same for everyone.
