The problem

A package sits with no scan. The first message the customer gets is their own complaint. A short delay note would have helped. Nobody wrote it in time.

What SI does

When a shipment has no scan after the wait you set, SI drafts a short delay email. The draft uses only the last scan, the order, and your wording. It does not invent a new delivery date. It waits in a queue for you.

Related in this library: Morning watch for late, stuck, and returned packages, Answering where-is-it questions from your records, A daily watch for low stock and stuck orders.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Shopify apparel store

    An order has no scan for five days. The draft says the last scan date and asks the customer to watch for the next one. The owner sends it.

  • Home-goods store

    A weather delay is on the scan. The draft repeats that line. It does not add a new arrival day.

  • Marketplace seller

    Orders that already have a customer email about the same shipment are skipped so you do not send two notes.

What it takes to set up

GemmiSI sets this up on your own equipment and accounts; your data stays with you. Give read access to orders and tracking. Save delay wording you already like. Permission is to draft, not to send. We sit with you, plan the steps, and teach you to run it. Setup is often a few days.
