Checking customer orders against their PO and payment

SI (Super Intelligence, the new name for AI) checks the paperwork before an order ships or work starts. It compares the customer's purchase order, your invoice, and their payment. It flags any mismatch.

The problem

Business customers send purchase orders. Then they pay by transfer with a reference. That reference may or may not match. Mismatched quantities, prices, and amounts cause billing disputes. They also cause delays.

What SI does

SI reads the customer's purchase order. It reads your sales order or invoice. It reads the payment record or remittance. It checks customer, quantities, prices, totals, tax, and payment amount. It lists mismatches. When everything matches, it drafts the order confirmation. When it does not, it drafts a clarifying question.

Examples

The same job looks different in different businesses. These are examples, not client results.

  • Industrial supply distributor

    A school district's purchase order lists a lower unit price than the invoice. The order is held. A clarification is drafted.

  • Uniform supplier

    A restaurant group's payment covers two of three invoices. The remittance is matched. The gap is noted.

  • Event rental company

    A corporate client's purchase order is matched to the quote and deposit. Then the delivery is scheduled.

What it takes to set up

Access to the inbox where purchase orders and remittances arrive. You also need invoices and bank or payment records. Setup is often a few days.

What a human still checks

  • A person releases the order or work after reviewing any mismatch.
  • A person approves confirmation and clarification emails before sending.
  • Payment records are read-only for SI.

Try it yourself

Paste this into your SI tool. Swap the [bracketed] parts for your own details. It asks SI to stop and show you before it does anything.

Copy-ready prompt 
For each order in [inbox/folder], compare the customer's purchase order with our invoice [file] and payment record [file]. Check customer, quantities, prices, totals, tax and amount paid. List mismatches. Draft a confirmation for matches and a clarifying question for mismatches. Pause and show me before sending, submitting, posting or changing anything.

Typical effect

Billing disputes are caught before the order ships. They are not found after the invoice is overdue.
